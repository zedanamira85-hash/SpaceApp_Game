SPACE MISSION DESIGN GAME

Europa Deep

Design a probe, explore the ocean beneath Europa’s ice, and send your findings back to Earth.

How to play

  1. Design. Pick a rocket, shielding, a power source, and up to three tools. Include the ice-melting drill.
  2. Check and launch. Keep cost and mass under their limits, meet the power and shielding requirements, and use E.V.A.’s tips to fix problems.
  3. Explore and transmit. Respond to an ocean event with your onboard tools, then send your findings home.

A fictional future mission inspired by NASA research.
